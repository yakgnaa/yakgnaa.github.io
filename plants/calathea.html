Common Calathea problems

Crispy brown edges

Symptoms: Dry, curling, crispy leaf margins.
Likely cause: Low humidity or tap water minerals/fluoride — Calatheas are notably sensitive.
What to do: Raise humidity with a humidifier or pebble tray, and water with filtered or distilled water.

Curling leaves

Symptoms: Leaves roll or curl inward.
Likely cause: Underwatering or low humidity.
What to do: Water more consistently and increase ambient humidity.

Spider mites

Symptoms: Fine webbing and speckled discoloration on leaves.
Likely cause: Dry air.
What to do: Raise humidity, rinse leaves, and treat with insecticidal soap if needed.

FAQ

Is Calathea toxic to cats and dogs?

No, Calatheas are considered non-toxic and are a popular pet-safe choice.

Why does my Calathea have crispy brown edges?

Almost always low humidity or minerals in tap water — try filtered water and a humidifier.

Why does my Calathea fold its leaves at night?

This is normal — Calatheas (“prayer plants”) raise and fold their leaves in a daily light-driven rhythm.

How much humidity does Calathea need?

Ideally 50% or higher; dry indoor air is the leading cause of crispy, unhappy leaves.
