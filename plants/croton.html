Common Croton problems

Sudden leaf drop

Symptoms: Multiple leaves dropping at once.
Likely cause: Crotons are very sensitive to change — cold drafts, being moved, or a sudden environment shift.
What to do: Keep it in a stable, warm spot with consistent light, away from drafts.

Fading color

Symptoms: The plant's bright red, orange, or yellow coloring fades to plain green.
Likely cause: Not enough light — croton needs bright light to hold its vivid color.
What to do: Move to a brighter spot, ideally with some direct sun.

Spider mites

Symptoms: Fine webbing and stippled, dull leaves.
Likely cause: Dry indoor air.
What to do: Raise humidity, rinse leaves, and treat with insecticidal soap if needed.

FAQ

Is croton toxic to cats and dogs?

Yes, its sap can irritate the mouth and stomach and cause vomiting if chewed or eaten.

Why is my croton losing its bright colors?

It needs more light — croton's vivid color depends on bright light, ideally with some direct sun.

Why is my croton dropping leaves?

It's very sensitive to change; check for cold drafts, a recent move, or a sudden shift in watering or light.

How much light does croton need?

As much bright light as you can give it, including some direct sun, to keep its color vivid.
