Common Rubber Plant problems

Root rot

Symptoms: Yellow, drooping leaves and a mushy stem base.
Likely cause: Overwatering.
What to do: Trim rotten roots, repot in a well-draining mix, and water less frequently.

Leaf drop

Symptoms: Lower leaves yellow and drop.
Likely cause: Overwatering, or a sudden change in light or temperature.
What to do: Check soil moisture, keep it away from cold drafts, and maintain consistent care.

Dull, dusty leaves

Symptoms: Leaves look dull and less glossy.
Likely cause: Dust buildup blocking light absorption — a cosmetic issue, not a disease.
What to do: Wipe leaves with a damp cloth periodically.

FAQ

Is a rubber plant toxic to cats and dogs?

Yes, its milky sap can irritate the mouth and stomach if chewed.

Why is my rubber plant dropping leaves?

Most often overwatering, or a sudden environmental change like cold drafts or being moved.

How often should I water a rubber plant?

About every 1–2 weeks, when the top 2 inches of soil have dried out.

How do I keep my rubber plant's leaves shiny?

Wipe them occasionally with a damp cloth to remove dust — this is cosmetic care, not a health issue.
