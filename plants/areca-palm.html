Common Areca Palm problems

Brown frond tips

Symptoms: Crispy brown tips on fronds.
Likely cause: Low humidity, tap water minerals/fluoride, or fertilizer salt buildup.
What to do: Use filtered water, raise humidity, and flush the soil occasionally.

Yellowing fronds

Symptoms: Whole fronds turn yellow.
Likely cause: Overwatering, poor drainage, or natural aging of older fronds.
What to do: Check drainage, let the soil dry slightly more between waterings, and trim old yellowed fronds.

Spider mites

Symptoms: Fine webbing and speckled fronds.
Likely cause: Dry indoor air.
What to do: Raise humidity, rinse fronds, and treat with insecticidal soap if widespread.

FAQ

Is an Areca palm toxic to cats and dogs?

No, it's considered non-toxic and is one of the most popular pet-safe palms.

Why does my Areca palm have brown tips?

Usually tap water minerals or fluoride, or low humidity — try filtered water and a humidifier.

How often should I water an Areca palm?

About once a week, keeping the soil lightly moist but not soggy.

Does Areca palm need bright light?

Yes, bright indirect light keeps it fullest and healthiest, though it tolerates medium light too.
