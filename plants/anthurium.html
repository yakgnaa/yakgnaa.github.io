Common Anthurium problems

Root rot

Symptoms: Yellow leaves, mushy roots, and wilting despite moist soil.
Likely cause: Overwatering or poor drainage.
What to do: Trim rotten roots, repot in a chunky, well-draining aroid mix, and water less often.

No blooms

Symptoms: Healthy leaves but few or no flower spathes.
Likely cause: Not enough light, or insufficient nutrients.
What to do: Move to brighter indirect light and feed lightly during the growing season.

Brown leaf tips

Symptoms: Crispy brown edges.
Likely cause: Low humidity or fertilizer salt buildup.
What to do: Raise humidity and flush the soil occasionally with plain water.

FAQ

Is Anthurium toxic to cats and dogs?

Yes, it contains calcium oxalate crystals that irritate the mouth and stomach if chewed.

Why won't my Anthurium bloom?

It usually needs brighter indirect light and regular light feeding during the growing season.

How often should I water an Anthurium?

About once a week, when the top inch of soil has dried out.

Why are my Anthurium's leaves turning yellow?

Most often overwatering or poor drainage causing root stress.
