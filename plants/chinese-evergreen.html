Common Chinese Evergreen problems

Root rot

Symptoms: Yellow, mushy lower leaves and a soft stem base.
Likely cause: Overwatering.
What to do: Trim rotten roots, repot in a well-draining mix, and cut back on watering.

Brown leaf edges

Symptoms: Crispy brown edges or tips.
Likely cause: Low humidity or tap water minerals.
What to do: Use filtered water and raise humidity.

Fading variegation

Symptoms: Colorful patterns turn plain green.
Likely cause: Not enough light.
What to do: Move to a brighter (still indirect) spot to preserve leaf coloring.

FAQ

Is Chinese evergreen toxic to cats and dogs?

Yes, it contains calcium oxalate crystals that irritate the mouth and stomach if chewed.

Why is my Chinese evergreen losing its color or variegation?

It likely needs more light — low light causes new leaves to grow plainer green.

How often should I water Chinese evergreen?

About once a week, keeping the soil lightly moist but not soggy.

Can Chinese evergreen grow in low light?

Yes, it's one of the more shade-tolerant houseplants, though variegated varieties keep their color better with more light.
