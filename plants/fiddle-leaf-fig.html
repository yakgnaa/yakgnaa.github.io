Common Fiddle Leaf Fig problems

Brown spots on leaves

Symptoms: Dark brown spots or patches, sometimes with a yellow halo.
Likely cause: Overwatering, inconsistent watering, or bacterial/fungal leaf spot.
What to do: Let the soil dry more between waterings, improve air circulation, and remove badly affected leaves.

Sudden leaf drop

Symptoms: Multiple leaves dropping at once, sometimes suddenly.
Likely cause: Fiddle leaf figs are very sensitive to change — cold drafts, being moved, or a sudden watering shift.
What to do: Keep it in a stable spot away from drafts or AC vents and maintain a consistent watering routine.

Spider mites

Symptoms: Fine webbing and speckled, yellowing leaves.
Likely cause: Dry indoor air.
What to do: Increase humidity, wipe leaves regularly, and treat with insecticidal soap or neem oil.

FAQ

Is fiddle leaf fig toxic to cats and dogs?

Yes, its sap contains irritants that can cause mouth and stomach irritation if chewed.

Why is my fiddle leaf fig dropping leaves?

Most often stress from a sudden change — a new location, cold draft, or a big shift in watering.

Why does my fiddle leaf fig have brown spots?

Usually overwatering or inconsistent watering causing fungal or bacterial leaf spot.

How much light does a fiddle leaf fig need?

Bright, indirect light — near an unobstructed window, but not scorching direct midday sun.
