Common Jade Plant problems

Root rot

Symptoms: Soft, mushy, discolored leaves and stems.
Likely cause: Overwatering.
What to do: Remove from the pot, trim rotten roots, repot in a well-draining succulent mix, and water far less often.

Shriveled, wrinkled leaves

Symptoms: Leaves look deflated or wrinkly.
Likely cause: Underwatering for an extended period.
What to do: Water thoroughly; leaves plump back up within days.

Leggy, stretched growth

Symptoms: Long thin stems reaching toward light, with sparse leaves.
Likely cause: Not enough light.
What to do: Move to a sunnier spot and prune to encourage a fuller, more compact shape.

FAQ

Is a jade plant toxic to cats and dogs?

Yes, it can cause vomiting, lethargy, and incoordination if ingested.

Why are my jade plant's leaves falling off?

Usually overwatering (soft, mushy leaves), or physical disturbance — jade leaves also drop easily when bumped.

How often should I water a jade plant?

About every 2–3 weeks, letting the soil dry out completely between waterings.

Why is my jade plant leggy?

It needs more direct light; move it to a sunnier window and prune to encourage denser growth.
