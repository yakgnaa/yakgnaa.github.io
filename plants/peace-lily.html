Common Peace Lily problems

Dramatic drooping

Symptoms: The whole plant wilts and droops noticeably.
Likely cause: Usually underwatering — peace lilies droop clearly when thirsty.
What to do: Water thoroughly; it typically perks back up within hours. Persistent drooping despite moist soil can instead mean root rot.

Brown leaf tips

Symptoms: Crispy brown tips or edges.
Likely cause: Tap water chemicals, low humidity, or fertilizer salt buildup.
What to do: Use filtered or distilled water, raise humidity, and flush the soil occasionally to clear salts.

No flowers

Symptoms: Healthy green growth but no white blooms.
Likely cause: Not enough light.
What to do: Move to a brighter spot with indirect light — peace lilies flower poorly in low light.

FAQ

Is peace lily toxic to cats and dogs?

Yes, it contains calcium oxalate crystals that irritate the mouth and digestive tract if chewed.

Why is my peace lily drooping?

Almost always thirst — it wilts noticeably when it needs water and recovers quickly afterward.

Why won't my peace lily bloom?

It likely needs more indirect light; low-light spots keep it green but rarely produce flowers.

How often should I water a peace lily?

About once a week, keeping the soil lightly moist but never soggy.
