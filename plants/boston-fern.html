Common Boston Fern problems

Crispy, browning fronds

Symptoms: Fronds turn dry, brown, and brittle.
Likely cause: Low humidity or underwatering — Boston ferns are very sensitive to dry air.
What to do: Increase humidity substantially (humidifier, pebble tray), and water more frequently.

Yellowing fronds

Symptoms: Fronds yellow and drop.
Likely cause: Overwatering, poor drainage, or natural shedding of old fronds.
What to do: Check drainage, remove yellowed fronds, and keep soil moist but not waterlogged.

Scale insects

Symptoms: Small brown bumps on the undersides of fronds.
Likely cause: Introduced from another plant.
What to do: Wipe off with an alcohol-soaked cotton swab, and treat with insecticidal soap if widespread.

FAQ

Is a Boston fern toxic to cats and dogs?

No, it's considered non-toxic and is a popular pet-safe hanging plant.

Why does my Boston fern keep turning brown and crispy?

Low humidity is the most common cause — Boston ferns need consistently high humidity to thrive indoors.

How often should I water a Boston fern?

Often — keep the soil consistently moist, checking every few days rather than on a long fixed schedule.

Why is my Boston fern dropping fronds?

Usually low humidity or inconsistent watering; some natural older-frond shedding is also normal.
